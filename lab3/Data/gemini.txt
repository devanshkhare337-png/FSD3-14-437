Google Gemini is a family of advanced artificial intelligence models developed by Google, serving both as a conversational AI assistant and as the underlying engine for many Google products. Unlike older AI systems that were trained strictly on text, Gemini was built from the ground up to be natively multimodal. This means it can seamlessly understand, process, and combine various types of information at the same time, including text, images, audio, video, and computer code.

At an everyday level, Gemini functions as a chatbot where users can ask questions, brainstorm ideas, translate languages, write essays, or generate custom images and videos. Beyond the standalone app, Google integrates these models directly into its broader ecosystem, bringing AI capabilities to Workspace tools like Gmail and Google Docs, as well as Google Search and Android devices.

The Gemini models are organized into different tiers based on computing power and purpose. Recent frontier models, such as the Gemini 3 series and Gemini 3.7 Flash, feature massive context windows capable of processing huge amounts of data—like entire books, codebases, or long videos—in a single prompt. Google has also focused heavily on equipping Gemini with "agentic" capabilities. Rather than just passively answering questions, these AI agents can interact with external tools, plan ahead, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously to help users solve complex problems or automate workflows.
